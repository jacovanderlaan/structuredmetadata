Key concept SQL & generation

Round Trip Engineering

Metadata flows into the editor as you write — not just out at deploy.

The counter to one-way generation: as you shape SQL in a notebook or annotate it SQL-first, that work flows back into the metadata, and metadata flows forward into your editing surface. Generation and authoring stay in sync in both directions, so hand-edits are first-class and drift is prevented.
